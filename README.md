# CS330-Computer-Graphics
SNHU CS 330 portfolio project showcasing a 3D OpenGL scene with textures, lighting, and interactive camera controls
CS 330: Computational Graphics and Visualization

Project Overview

This repository contains my final project for CS 330: Computational Graphics and Visualization at Southern New Hampshire University. The project involved creating an interactive 3D scene using C++ and OpenGL. My scene features a speaker, a coffee mug, and additional objects arranged on a flat surface. I incorporated textures, lighting, and camera controls to create a more realistic and interactive environment.

The repository includes my completed 3D scene ZIP file and Design Decisions document, which explain the development process and the choices I made throughout the project.

How Do I Approach Designing Software?

Throughout this project, I developed a better understanding of how to plan and design software before beginning implementation. One of the most important skills I gained was learning how to break complex objects into smaller, manageable components. For example, I created my speaker and coffee mug using basic 3D shapes, including cylinders, spheres, and a torus. I also learned how to use transformations, such as scaling, rotation, and translation, to position objects correctly within a scene.

My design process involved selecting objects, identifying the shapes needed to construct them, and gradually refining their appearance. I started with basic geometry before incorporating textures, lighting, and camera functionality. This approach helped me focus on one component at a time instead of trying to complete the entire scene at once.

In future projects, I can apply these design strategies by breaking larger requirements into smaller tasks, planning the structure of an application before coding, and evaluating my design throughout development.

How Do I Approach Developing Programs?

Working on my 3D scene helped me develop new programming strategies, particularly when working with C++ and OpenGL. I gained experience modifying existing code, working with shader programs, applying textures, and implementing lighting and camera controls. I also became more comfortable troubleshooting build errors and identifying issues within a larger codebase.

Iteration played a significant role in my development process. Throughout the project, I repeatedly adjusted the size, position, and appearance of objects to improve the overall scene. For example, I modified the speaker's proportions and placement, adjusted the coffee mug and its handle, and experimented with lighting to achieve the desired appearance. Some changes did not produce the results I expected, so I had to review my code, make adjustments, and test again.

As I progressed through the milestones, my development approach became more organized. Initially, I focused on getting individual objects to appear correctly. Later, I began considering how different components worked together, including textures, lighting, and user interaction. By the end of the project, I had developed a stronger understanding of incremental development, testing, and debugging.

How Can Computer Science Help Me in Reaching My Goals?

Computer science continues to provide me with the technical knowledge and problem-solving skills needed to pursue a career in software engineering. Although I do not currently plan to specialize in computer graphics, this course introduced me to programming concepts that can be applied across many areas of software development.

From an educational perspective, working with computational graphics strengthened my understanding of C++, mathematical transformations, program structure, and debugging. It also helped me see how different components of an application interact to produce a complete result. These skills will be valuable as I continue studying software engineering and working on more advanced programming projects.

From a professional perspective, this project reinforced the importance of patience, adaptability, and continuous improvement. Software development often involves troubleshooting unexpected problems, evaluating different solutions, and refining code until it meets the intended requirements. Creating my 3D scene gave me practical experience with this process and helped me become more confident working on complex technical projects.

Overall, CS 330 challenged me to learn unfamiliar tools and techniques while building a functional application. I am proud of the progress I made throughout the course, and I plan to carry these skills into future coursework and my career as a software engineer.
